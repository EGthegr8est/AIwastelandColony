WASTELAND COLONY
A post-nuclear colony sandbox. Coded with AI by Eric B.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (Desktop is fine). Don't run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click WastelandColony.exe.
3. Click "New colony", pick a leader, and go.

There is nothing to install, and no other program is needed.

WINDOWS WARNING ("Windows protected your PC")
The game isn't code-signed (that costs money), so Windows may show a blue warning the
first time. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Some antivirus programs also complain
about new, unsigned programs; it's a false alarm. If one blocks it, allow it.

WHAT YOU NEED
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a graphics card from the last 8 years or so (it needs Vulkan).

THE BASICS
- Pan the map: WASD / arrow keys, or right-drag. Zoom: mouse wheel.
- F11 goes fullscreen, and F11 again goes back. The game fills whatever size the window is.
- Left-click a tile to look at it, claim it or build on it.
- END SEASON (top right) moves time on. The game autosaves every season. The map then plays the
  season's walking back, so you watch your parties go; a click, Space or Enter skips it, and the
  WALK button beside END SEASON sets it to Normal, Fast or Off.
- Menu (top right) goes back to the title screen: Resume, Load, Quit.
- Click a scouting party to pick it, then click somewhere to see how far its food goes.
  Esc lets go.
- Deer and bears wander the wastes. Click one and send hunters (two dice rolls: track it, then
  the kill shot). Bears attack parties that come too close. Something huge sleeps under the
  biggest ruined city: scavenging near it is a gamble, and its hide makes Salt (build a Drug Factory).
- The ART button switches the map between four pixel-art styles (4, the clean look, is the default) and old-school text.
- Hover the mouse over a tile to see what it is.
- Every season the map fades between day and night (Spring and Autumn are day). Craters, camps you can see and ruins
  held by raiders keep glowing in the dark. The Legend tab has a Night On/Off switch.
- The river is a wall: only boats and planes cross it, plus the shallow fords, the old bridge in the middle of the map (one tile wide) and any bridge you build out from your bank.
- Rough ground (rock, forest, ruins, dunes) slows a party, and climbing rock or steep slopes can break a leg: the party slows until it is home.
- A ruined highway (the Hollow Mile) runs through the cities and over the old bridge, but raiders ambush people on it. A picture comes up the first
  time your scouts see it (a grey overcast picture by day; by night an orange dusk one, or a green one if the finders are in high radiation). Build roads (stone and grain) to reach it: anyone can use a road, the Rust Kings too, and they build their own. Select a
  tile of the highway beside your road to repair it into a road for 1 stone, which ends the ambushes there.
- Glowing craters can be searched once, for scrap or a live nuke: a natural 1 sets it off (the party dies and the 3 by 3 square turns to hot ash), a natural 20 disarms it.
  The missile silo can hold up to three. A nuke is very heavy: a party on foot lifts one, horses two, a war rig three. Research Strategic bombers (Warfare, tier 5), build
  a Bomber at an Airfield, then click any tile and press Drop a nuke here (twice, to confirm). Everything in the 3 by 3 square dies, yours too.
  A trader camp very rarely has a warhead for sale (500 gold, half for a Trader leader): send a caravan on horseback or in a war rig, and get it home.
  Late in the game the Rust Kings build an airfield and a bomber of their own and will nuke your buildings: Watchtowers warn you, Flak towers shoot the bomber down,
  and if your scouts destroy their airfield they cannot launch until they rebuild it.
- The ruined gas station always stands beside the old highway.
- The MUSIC slider is in the top bar (and on the menu screens). All the way down is off.

YOUR SAVES
They live in your user folder, not in the game folder:
    %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Wasteland Colony\
To remove the game, delete the game folder. To wipe saves too, delete that folder as well.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
Open a command prompt in the game folder and run:
    WastelandColony.exe -- --selftest
and send Eric what it prints (it checks that the art, music and data all loaded).
